# HAUNTED (Official name pending)

## GAME DESCRIPTION

You are a journalist in the mid 70s, who have a strong fascination for unsolved mysteries. 

## CHARACTERS (all names can be changed later)

### Protagonist

A journalist for the Macondo Gazette.

### Martinez Family

A young couple with a little child. Nobody has seen them since a month ago. They just vanished.

### Martinez family neighbors on 301

A big family with a really loud dog. They can provide some useful information if you ask right.

### Martinez family neighbor on 303

An senior person, that become really close to the family since they move in.

## LOCATIONS (All names can be changed later)

### Macondo County

Where the whole game takes place. It's a big town in the mid 70s. Where some strange things are happening, people are disappearing since the past few months, and the police is not doing enough about it.

### Bosque Avenue 2400 Building

The place where our story start.

### Bosque Avenue 2400, Apartment 303

The home of the Martinez family.

### Slaughter house

An old abandoned slaughter house. The company that used to own it, when bankrupt 5 years ago.

### Macondo Gazette Building

Where our protagonist work.

## CHAPTERS

### Chapter 1: A family is missing

#### Location

Bosque Avenue 2400, Apartment 303

#### Summary

You got into a building where there is an apartment where a family used to live, but since a month ago, no one has seen them. The police filled a report, but they are not taking it seriously, because they believe the family just left the town.

#### Primary Mission

You should find something that could give you a clue about the current family's whereabouts.

#### Secondary Missions

- Maybe some of the neighbor knows more about the family than you think. You should try to get some information from them, but be careful who you ask, because most of them are not very friendly and you are not supposed to be there.

#### Objectives (not visible for the player)

- The apartment door is locked, but you can force it with your lock picking skills.
- Once you are inside the apartment, you should look around to find some clues.
- You have to take some pictures.
- Also, you should try to ask some of the neighbors what they know about the family.

### Chapter 2: Unsolved murder

#### Location

Bolivar Street 231

#### Summary

A neighbor of Martinez family has given you a very important piece of information that leads you to the next location. Now you are outside a house where a murder was committed recently. A dead body was found by the police, with a lot of wounds in the abdomen, that could be made with a kitchen knife. There is no trace of the murderer yet.

### Chapter 3: Strange noises

#### Summary

Your boss told you that they can't publish your article about the Martinez family and the connection with the murder in Bolivar Street 231, because you trespassed into private property twice. Instead of publishing the article, he wants you to go to an old house where the current residents have been hearing strange and disturbing noises lately. They think that the house cold be haunted by the ghosts of the previous owners.
