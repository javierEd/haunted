## CHAPTERS

### Chapter 1: A family is missing

#### Location

Bosque Avenue 2400, Apartment 303

#### Summary

You are in a building where there is an apartment where a family used to live, but since a month ago, no one has seen them. The police filled a report, but they are not taking it seriously, because they believe the family just left the town.

#### Primary Mission

You should find something that could give you a clue about the current family's whereabouts.

#### Secondary Missions

- Maybe some of the neighbor knows more about the family than you think. You should try to get some information from them.

#### Objectives (not visible for the player)

- The apartment door is locked, but the neighbor in the apartment 305 has a spare key, you must talk to him first and convince him to give you the key.
- Once you have the key, you must unlock the door and get in the apartment.
- Also, you should try to ask all the neighbors what they know about the family.
